System design · end to end

Data rises. Requests descend. The verdict sits in the middle, and the AI cannot reach it.

A language model is excellent at understanding a badly-phrased question in Tamil and writing a clear answer. It is also capable of confidently writing “conditions look fine” over a 2.6 m sea when the boat's limit is 1.5 m.

So ORCA separates deciding from explaining and puts a wall between them. Everything else in this diagram follows from that one decision.

The rule that shows the philosophy. Layer legends are generated from the same lookup tables that colour the pixels, so a colour means a temperature. A visual treatment breaks that — so while one is active the legends hide themselves and say why. A legend that no longer matches the pixels is a specific false claim.

Two endpoints behave unusually, on purpose. The agent stream pushes reasoning in pieces with buffering disabled, compression off and a heartbeat, because a trace that arrives in one lump at the end proves nothing. The route planner returns success for a refusal — “no safe passage, and here are the six cells that block it” is a complete answer, not an error to retry.

English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Odia, Konkani. All eleven voices were verified by generating audio rather than trusting the documentation — which is how we found that Odia's documented voice is rejected by the live service and that Konkani has no native voice at all. ORCA marks the substitute rather than passing a Marathi voice off as Konkani.

Why the guard exists. Asked to translate an advisory beginning “NO-GO”, the service returned Tamil meaning “punishment for those who go astray”. Asked to translate “wind = 14.7 kn”, it returned “14.7 kilometres” — every digit intact, the quantity wrong. So numerals, verdict words, bearings and confusable units are masked, translated, re-injected verbatim, and verified as a multiset. A clause that cannot be verified stays in English rather than being translated unsafely.

Each tool publishes a machine-readable description of itself — what it answers, its resolution, its speed, its provenance, its cost — and the planner chooses from those. Selecting everything is treated as a wrong answer: it wastes time and shows no judgement. The plan is published before anything runs, which is what makes the trace checkable rather than a summary written afterwards.

Compound questions are split into labelled sub-questions, and the tools each label needs become a floor the planner may add to but not drop from — because without that it answered “is it safe?” and silently ignored “and where are the fish?”.

The critic can reject its own side's work. If a draft softens a NO-GO or quotes a figure absent from the fetched data, it is sent back. The interface shows the round count: a rejection is displayed, not hidden.

Four measurements weighted into one index out of 100 — wave 35%, wind 30%, lightning 20%, visibility 15% — judged against the limits for your boat class, versioned as orca-thresholds-2026.08 and displayed on every verdict.

Then hard vetoes are checked separately, and they override the index. If exceeding the wave limit were merely a heavy penalty, calm wind and clear visibility could out-vote it and produce a GO in a sea that would sink the boat. Weighted averages have no concept of “never”, so “never” is a separate mechanism that runs afterwards. The same reasoning appears in the router, where a forbidden cell is made impassable rather than expensive.

The wall against the AI is enforced four independent ways: the field recording who decided carries a type that can hold only "rule_engine"; an automated test forbids this folder from importing the agent folder at all; the critic checks the draft against the verdict; and the interface states it on screen with the thresholds version.

One grid for the whole system: 60°E–100°E, 0°N–25°N at 0.05° — 400,000 cells about 5.5 km across — so every layer can be compared cell by cell. Fishing zones are re-derived from satellite temperature and chlorophyll because INCOIS publishes its advisories as picture maps, and software cannot read a picture of a map.

The convention trap. Wind direction is where the wind comes from; current direction is where the water flows to. Opposite conventions for the same-looking number, and getting one backwards gives a field that looks perfectly plausible and points exactly the wrong way. Handled in one function that takes the convention as an argument — because writing the sign flip twice is how the two ended up agreeing when they should not have.

ORCA owns no sensors, so a large part of the engineering is behaving well as a guest on other people's systems. A source being down returns a result marked as failed rather than crashing the request, and every success and failure feeds the “sources 6/7” readout — which is a measurement, not a decoration.

The worst bug in the project's history. Ocean current speed was being read as metres per second while the service returned knots — a factor of two, silently, on a value that feeds routing and search-and-rescue drift. The rule that came out of it: never assume a unit; read the one the response declares and convert — and if the conversion is not physically meaningful, mark the value UNAVAILABLE rather than passing it through with a hopeful label.

The operational adapters now call Copernicus Marine, NASA CMR/POWER, Sentinel Hub, AISStream, Global Fishing Watch and CelesTrak. Each integration reports its real mode and caveat through /integrations/status; catalogue discovery, live observations, model values and derived predictions are never presented as the same thing.

  • provenance.py

No number travels through ORCA as a bare number. Every value carries its dataset, provider, unit, provenance state, when it was valid, whether that is stale for its kind, what it was derived from, and its citation. Two checks run at the moment a value is created, and a value that fails them is not created — the program raises an error instead of quietly carrying an unattributed number:

— A DERIVED value with no declared inputs is rejected. This is why the fishing-zone layer cannot be magic: it is structurally obliged to name its sources.

— An UNAVAILABLE value that nonetheless carries a number is rejected. “We could not get it” and “here is the figure” contradict each other, and the type refuses to hold both.

The consequence. Because UNAVAILABLE is a real state rather than an absence, ORCA has a fourth verdict: UNVERIFIABLE — “could not obtain enough data to judge; this is not the same as safe”, with the missing inputs named. Most systems do not have this verdict.

Waves, period, direction, swell, sea temperature, currents, wind, gusts, visibility, rainfall, CAPE and CIN — plus Indian ocean products, boundary geometry and Earth imagery. Four language-model providers are tried in order, and if all four fail ORCA writes the answer from a fixed template: less fluent, equally correct, still cited.

Two details worth knowing. Open-Meteo's free tier is metered per location, not per request, so a 300-point query spends 300 of the allowance — ORCA meters itself on its own side rather than learning the limit by being refused. And INCOIS's certificate chain is incomplete: the lazy fix is to switch off encryption verification, so ORCA ships the missing intermediate certificate and completes the chain properly instead.

Runs on nothing

SQLite and an in-process cache by default. No Docker, no Postgres, no Redis, no cloud account. PostGIS and Redis are optional upgrades detected at startup, and a dead accelerator is a logged fallback rather than an outage — with tests asserting it. The demo has to survive a judge's laptop.

Three journeys through the spine

The fastest way to understand a system is to follow one request all the way through.

Tap a point on the sea

layers 9 → 3 → 2 → 5 → 9 ≈ 1.5 s
01

Both requests go together

Conditions and verdict are fetched in one shot. Fetched separately they could disagree — and a verdict that contradicts the evidence below it is worse than no verdict.

02

Two services, in parallel

Waves and currents from a marine model; wind, visibility and thunderstorm energy from an atmospheric one. Asked simultaneously, because the delay is visible to the user.

03

Each value is wrapped

Source, declared unit, valid time, and whether that makes it stale for its kind. A value the service could not supply becomes UNAVAILABLE rather than silently vanishing.

04

The rule engine decides

Boat length selects a class, the class supplies the limits, four measurements are scored and weighted, hard vetoes are checked separately. One of four verdicts, with the arithmetic recorded.

05

Confidence, judged separately

A missing measurement scores zero and forces low confidence — because “we could not measure the waves” and “the waves are calm” must never produce the same output.

06

The verdict, and the receipt

Every number with its provenance badge — including whether it is a forecast for a future hour rather than a stale reading. A value valid at 06:00 tomorrow is 14 h “old” by subtraction and perfectly current in fact.

Ask a compound question in Tamil, by voice

layers 9 → 7 → 6 → 5 → 7 → 9 ≈ 15 s, all of it visible
01

Speak, and see the level move

The meter is driven by the measured microphone signal, not an animation, so a muted mic visibly does nothing. Audio is converted in-browser to the 16 kHz mono the engines expect.

02

ORCA shows what it heard

It does not send it straight on. Recognition on a question containing a place name is the least reliable link in the chain, so the transcript is shown for correction — and the detected language is checked against the one selected.

03

Split into two questions

“Is it safe tomorrow?” and “where will I find fish?” are separated and labelled. The tools each label needs become a floor. You watch this happen.

04

The plan, then the tools

Five tools with the reason each was chosen — published before execution. Then each result lands with the real time it took.

05

Verdict, then prose

The rule engine's answer is handed to the model as unchangeable. The model writes English covering both halves in the order asked. Then the critic rules on it.

06

Translated clause by clause

English appears immediately; Tamil follows as a separate delivery. Each clause is verified independently, and one that cannot be verified stays in English. The panel reports “figures intact · 18”.

07

It speaks a summary

The verdict and the reason that drove it. The full compound answer synthesised to 66 seconds of Tamil, and nobody listens to 66 seconds before launching a boat. Whole lines are dropped, never half a figure.

Plan a passage — including the refusal

layers 9 → 5 → 3 → 5 → 9 ≈ 3 s
01

A corridor becomes a grid

Cells about 28 km across. The cell count is capped by how many questions ORCA may ask the weather service per minute — not by an arbitrary number.

02

Every cell asks the same engine

Not a simplified copy — the identical function that produced the on-screen verdict. The router carries no thresholds of its own, so there is exactly one definition of “unsafe”.

03

Land is the model's own silence

The marine service returns nothing for wave height over land. A cell with no wave height is one the wave model declines to answer for — which is exactly the set a boat must not cross, and more accurate than any coastline outline at this scale.

04

A* over the costed grid

Cost is distance × a penalty that grows with risk, squared so a slightly worse cell is barely avoided and a nearly-forbidden one is avoided hard. A test compares the result against exhaustive search — optimality is verified, not asserted.

05

Or: no route, and why

ORCA names the specific cells that block it, with the reason for each. “No route found” alone is indistinguishable from a broken planner — and the refusal is the more convincing demonstration.

What ORCA refuses to do

These are decisions, not gaps, and they are as much a part of the architecture as anything above.

Issue warnings

The IMD and INCOIS do that, with authority ORCA does not have. The machine-readable export is shaped to feed their systems — CAP 1.2, the format NDMA SACHET already publishes in.

Let the AI decide

Enforced four separate ways so three can fail and the rule still holds. The model explains a verdict; it never issues or softens one.

Message anyone

No SMS, no push, no email. ORCA has no authority to contact a fisherman, and a prototype that quietly acquires a notification channel is one that can spam a real person.

Guess where a missing person is

A search area with a stated containment fraction — 50% and 95% — never a point. A search that concentrates on one point because software offered one is worse than no software.

Let simulated data touch a verdict

Barred by the type system, drawn hatched, never silently mixed with live measurements.

Disable encryption to make a call work

The one-line fix for INCOIS's incomplete certificate chain is to stop checking. ORCA ships the missing intermediate instead.

Not built yet

Stated by us rather than found by you. The highest-value item is first.

next

A CIN gate on the lightning veto

Lightning is derived from CAPE because India has no free authoritative lightning feed. CAPE is potential, so in monsoon ORCA refuses almost the whole coast. High CAPE with strong convective inhibition means the atmosphere is capped and storms will not fire — ORCA already fetches CIN and does not yet use it. The most valuable unbuilt thing in the system.

partial

Persistence layer

PostGIS is probed and the in-memory fallback works, but the repository layer is unwritten — so nothing survives a restart. Traces, alerts and watched positions all live in memory.

built

Second-source adapters

Copernicus Marine, NASA Earthdata/POWER, Sentinel Hub, AISStream and Global Fishing Watch are callable and tested. Catalogue results remain metadata, AIS zero-counts retain their coverage caveat, and GFW remains delayed apparent activity.

built

Broader cross-validation

ORCA now validates SST, wind and significant wave height across independent sources after unit conversion and valid-time alignment. It reports agree, disagree, inconclusive or unavailable and never silently averages disagreement.

later

Authority dashboard

Today's console is single-vessel. A fisheries officer needs a fleet view: many boats, aggregate risk, who is in trouble. The data model supports it; the interface does not exist.

built

Satellite overpass · SGP4

Current CelesTrak elements are propagated for Sentinel-3A/B and EOS-06 and shown in the operational-intelligence panel. The result is explicitly a nominal-swath opportunity, not confirmation of acquisition or usable cloud cover.

later

Multi-leg trip planner

Single-leg routing works. “Leave at 04:00, fish here six hours, return before the wind builds” needs routing through time as well as space.

partial

Offline mode

Layers, reference geography and Earth imagery are cached and work with no network. The graceful degradation is real; the banner telling you about it is not built.

later

Langfuse observability

Recording AI behaviour over time — which prompts are slow, how often the critic rejects a draft. Credentials configured and dormant. Matters for operating at scale, little for a demonstration.

The claim, in one sentence. ORCA answers a fisherman's question in his own language and out loud, shows every number's source and age, computes the go / no-go with a fixed rule engine the artificial intelligence is not permitted to overrule, and says “I cannot tell” when it cannot — which is the only version of this product that is safe to put in front of a real person.

ORCA supplements, never replaces, official IMD and INCOIS bulletins. Safety-of-life-adjacent decision support, not a certified marine safety system. Real deployment needs validation against historical incident data and sign-off from a maritime safety authority.
